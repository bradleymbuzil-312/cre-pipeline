The residence becomes a whole-home suite, a product the hotel cannot offer today, and is counted as the 58th key. Its 24 slips take the marina from 23 slips today to 47 on completion.

It is underwritten conservatively — a $1,500 nightly rate at 35% occupancy, or $191,625 of annual revenue from stabilization.

Why It Matters

The parcel was part of the hotel until it was separated and sold to a third party during a bankruptcy roughly ten years ago. Acquiring it reassembles the original assemblage and takes the last private parcel between the hotel and its waterfront off the market.

The $1,100,000 purchase is funded from LP equity at closing, with no acquisition debt against the parcel. The $300,000 renovation is held back by the senior lender at the refinance and drawn against the work.

The estimated values in this package — $21,400,000 as is, $26,000,000 upon completion and $29,500,000 upon stabilization — are for the hotel and do not include this parcel. Every basis, coverage and return figure is measured against them, so what the acquisition is worth is upside those numbers do not carry.

Capitalize the EBITDA the parcel itself produces — the marina and room revenue — at 7.0% and it is worth roughly $4.3 million against a $1.4 million all-in cost.

Small Luxury Hotels affiliation and distribution.

Why the Affiliation Matters
Hilton Distribution

SLH member hotels are bookable directly through Hilton.com, the Hilton app and Hilton Honors. The affiliation places the property inside Hilton’s reservation and loyalty system rather than leaving it dependent on direct booking and third-party channels, which is the mechanism behind the projected occupancy and rate gain.

An Established Relationship

The sponsor already operates within SLH. Wylder Windham, the sister hotel in the Catskills, is an SLH member and was the first in that market. The application here is an extension of a working relationship rather than a first approach.

American Express Fine Hotels & Resorts

Wylder Windham carries the American Express Fine Hotels & Resorts designation, reached through the SLH platform. The same designation is the plan at Tilghman Island, adding a high-rate corporate and affluent leisure channel on completion.

Affiliation is targeted on completion of the renovation and remains subject to application, inspection, board approval and an executed brand affiliate agreement.
