A 57-key waterfront resort on 8.10 acres.

Wylder Hotel Tilghman Island sits on Tilghman Island in Talbot County, Maryland, between the Chesapeake Bay and the Choptank River: an 8.10-acre waterfront parcel with 1,100 feet of water frontage, a marina, pool, restaurant and boat bar. Operated under the Wylder Hotels brand, the property is mid-repositioning.

Ownership and operating structure.

The property is owned by 21551 Tilghman Investors LLC, led by principal John Flannigan, and operated under the Wylder Hotels brand through affiliate Wylder Eastern Shore Management, LLC, controlling day-to-day operations and the renovation.

Structure and Alignment
01Owner-operated, no third-party manager

Day-to-day operations are run by an affiliate of ownership. There is no third-party management agreement to terminate or subordinate, no comfort letter to negotiate and no split incentive between owner and operator.

02Continuous ownership since 2017

The sponsor has owned and operated the hotel under the Wylder brand through the pandemic cycle and the current repositioning, investing a further $642,693 of capital between 2021 and 2025.

03Single-asset ownership entity

21551 Tilghman Investors LLC holds this property and no other operating assets, keeping the asset and its cash flow ring-fenced.

04Two-property brand platform

Revenue management, distribution, design and procurement are shared across Wylder Hotels in Maryland and New York, giving a 57-key asset reach it could not support standing alone.

Reporting

Operating statements are produced monthly at the property level by the manager. The sponsor has held and operated the asset through a full market cycle under the same brand and the same management company, so the operating history and the forward plan come from one source.

Business plan and capital improvement program.

01
Guest room renovation

Full renovation of all 57 guest rooms at $25,000 per key. Four out-of-service keys are returned and three added in the back house, taking the hotel from 50 to 57: 12.3% more sellable inventory that has never produced revenue.

$1.43M
02
Add revenue streams

A rebuilt marina, the reimagined boat bar and a replaced 3-bedroom house broaden revenue beyond the room block.

$0.85M
03
Lift rate through product

A fully renovated room block, repositioned toward weddings and group events, closes the rate gap to the comp set.

Rate + Group

Exterior and interior improvements, contingency and professional fees complete the $3,003,000 program, which starts January 4, 2027 and completes in late June 2027.

Brand Optionality

A Small Luxury Hotels membership proposal is in hand and can be put into effect once the renovation is complete. The plan above does not depend on it; it is available to the repositioned product rather than to the hotel as it stands today.
