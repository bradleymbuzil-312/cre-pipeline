# Wylder and Dogwood Cove Marina Valuation

Oct 30, 2026 · @Bradley M. Buzil

## Conclusion

**Recommendation: $30,000 per slip, or $690,000 for the 23 Wylder slips, $720,000 for the 24 Dogwood Cove slips and $1,410,000 for the combined 47 slip marina, with a range of $25,000 to $40,000 per slip ($1,175,000 to $1,880,000 combined).** The Dogwood Cove figure assumes its slips are approved for commercial use with the hotel; until then I would carry them at about $280,000, the contract price less the parcel's assessed value.

The comp set is now seven deed verified marina sales within roughly an hour's drive of Tilghman, plus Dogwood Cove's own deed history. The three sales that are mostly slips (DiMillo's in Oxford, Gateway Marina in Trappe and Mears Point at Kent Narrows) run $22,100 to $40,800 per slip, or $26,800 to $48,000 adjusted to today at 3% a year, with a median of $30,800. The recommendation sits on that median. The four sales that carry a full service boatyard or hotel and restaurant operations trade at $94,000 to $184,000 per slip; they show what bay frontage with a business on it commands locally, but they are not slip prices.

The income approach on market seasonal rents supports $27,000 per slip, about 10% below the recommendation. I weight the local sales more heavily because they price this specific shoreline.

The sponsor model's 2028 slip revenue of $359,296 would support $2.7M to $3.5M for the combined marina, or $58,000 to $75,000 per slip. That is upside to be proven with a trailing marina P&L, not collateral value today.

All values cover the slips and their riparian rights only. They exclude the hotel, the Dogwood Cove residence and any upland business value, and they are my estimates, not an appraisal. Neither the LWHA nor the Newmark report allocates value to the marina.

### Reading the comps

- **Slip sales set the price.** The subject slips are hotel amenity slips with no boatyard, so the slip focused trades are the direct evidence: $26,800 to $48,000 per slip after time adjustment.
- **Location supports the middle of that range.** The two Talbot County slip sales (Oxford and Trappe) average $39,400 adjusted. Mears Point at $26,800 reflects a 540 slip volume discount that does not apply to 23 or 24 slips.
- **Time adjustment is conservative.** Two local resales show appreciation: Bachelor Point rose 3.4% a year from 2015 to 2025, and St. Michaels rose 11.4% a year from 2019 to 2021. I used 3% a year.
- **Business value is local and real but separate.** Lowe's Wharf, ten minutes from the subject, sold at $184,000 per slip because the price bought a restaurant, rooms and a house. Bachelor Point and Bay Bridge prove what institutional buyers pay for full service yards. None of that transfers to amenity slips.
- **Dogwood Cove needs its approvals.** The deed history and assessment say the market prices those slips today as residential piers. They earn hotel slip value only once Talbot County and MDE approve commercial use.
- **Net:** $30,000 per slip, the adjusted median, with $25,000 to $40,000 as the range.

## Income Approach

Two cases, both annual, stabilized, direct capitalization, no discounting.

### Market rent case

Transient rates in St. Michaels run about $2.60 to $3.95 per foot per night.

## Risks and Open Items

- **Slip counts.** Confirm 23 and 24 against a survey, the Dogwood Cove PSA and the riparian permits. Every value above scales linearly with the count.
- **Commercial use of Dogwood Cove slips.** Using the 24 slips commercially as part of the hotel needs Talbot County zoning, Critical Area and MDE riparian confirmation. Requires local legal verification. This is the difference between about $280,000 and $720,000.
- **Marina rebuild.** $250,000 on the PIP line is about $10,900 per existing slip. Confirm scope, permits and whether the 23 slips are rebuilt or replaced at 25.
- **Historical marina revenue.** The three year marina schedule promised in earlier lender Q&A is still the key document. It decides whether the model case is credible.
- **2028 slip expense.** The model types $17,965 (5%) against a stated 10% ratio.
- **Cap rate and expenses.** Both are assumptions. A marina specialist broker opinion or the appraiser's input would firm them up.
- **Comp verification.** Deed prices are from the Maryland SDAT assessment file. Multiple parcel deeds may include land beyond the marina, and the Gateway Marina match (SDAT 1601 Marina Dr; directory 1606 Marina Dr) should be confirmed. Slip counts come from press releases and directories, not surveys. The time adjustment rate is my assumption.
