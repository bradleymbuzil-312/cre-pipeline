Section 01

Transaction Summary

Piney Shores Resorts
Conroe, TX

Marcus and Millichap Capital Corporation has been exclusively engaged by Brookside Equities Inc. to arrange bridge financing for Piney Shores Resorts, a lakefront multifamily and mixed use asset at 8350 Piney Shores Drive in Conroe, Texas. The former Holiday Inn Piney Shores Presidential Resort carries a basis of $8,160,000 against a total project cost of $13,447,693 inclusive of a $3,550,000 renovation. The request totals $10,085,769, or 75% of total cost, against $3,361,923 of sponsor equity.

The loan is structured as a 24 month interest only bridge facility priced at SOFR plus 400 (7.66% all-in), with a 1.00% origination fee and a non recourse structure preferred. Approximately $9,677,416 funds at closing, with a $408,354 interest reserve held back to service the debt through the sell out. The business plan is to complete the renovation, map the 70 Presidential units as condominiums, and sell them out over four phases.

The base case is the condominium sell out: $18,340,000 of unit sales plus $1,400,000 of deeded boat slips, or $18,444,300 net of sales costs, retiring the loan on the 37th of 70 closings and returning a $4,996,607 net profit. The downside is apartment stabilization at $725,733 of Year 3 net operating income and an $11,600,000 value at a 6.25% capitalization rate, further supported by roughly $7,700,000 of additional collateral across excess land, residential lots, and separately saleable commercial buildings.

Section 09 · Exit

Condominium Conversion — Roadmap

Tex. Prop. Code Ch. 82
Montgomery County, TX
1
Feasibility & Title Work
Pull the title commitment and read Schedule B first. Clear deed restrictions and POA covenants, secure existing-lender consent with a partial-release mechanism, and resolve easements and recorded shoreline reservations.
2
Confirm Jurisdiction
City vs. unincorporated county governs the path. Montgomery County’s 2025 Development Regulations reference condos as a “Special Lot Product” — a pre-development meeting with the County Engineer fixes whether a plat or replat is required.
3
Tenant Notice
Tex. Prop. Code § 82.160 requires 60-day written conversion notice to each residential tenant, certified mail. Cannot be shortened; sequence the conversion around lease expirations.
4
Survey, Architect & Separation
§ 82.059 condo plat and plans set unit boundaries (§ 82.052). Dock, slip and bulkhead map as limited common elements. Separate water, sewer, electric and septic; add fire separation.
5
Governing Documents
Declaration (§ 82.055), unit owners’ association (§ 82.101) with two-owner deadlock mechanics, operating budget and reserves, master insurance (§ 82.111), and management certificate (§ 82.116). Lienholder joins and consents.
6
Record
Declaration, plat, plans and lender joinder record in the Montgomery County Real Property Records — the regime exists on recording. Split the MCAD tax account into two.
7
Disclosure Package
Condominium information statement (§ 82.153) plus conversion additions (§ 82.154: architect condition report, useful-life, code-violation list). Cancellation right (§ 82.156), deposit escrow (§ 82.158), lien release (§ 82.159).
8
Sell
New per-unit title commitments, partial releases from the existing lender, and closings.

Location & Market

Conroe, TX · Lake Conroe
Montgomery County

Conroe is the county seat of Montgomery County and one of the fastest growing cities in the country, ranked the number one fastest growing city in the Houston MSA in 2025 and 20th nationally. Located about 40 miles north of Houston on Interstate 45, it pairs direct metro connectivity with a lower cost of living and a pro business, no income tax environment.

Lake Conroe is a 21,000 acre recreational reservoir and premier lifestyle destination supporting year round boating, fishing, and water recreation. True lakefront multifamily inventory is scarce, and waterfront assets command premium rents and values relative to inland product. The southeast shore location places the asset closer to Houston than most of the lake.
