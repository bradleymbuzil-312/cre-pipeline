Approval Rights

Prior written consent required for a sale or refinancing that fails to repay the senior and redeem the preferred in full, additional debt or equity, change of control, structural or business-plan changes, and replacement of the manager or franchise.

Remedies on Default

Remove and replace the managing member, assume control of the business plan and renovation, replace the property manager, force a sale, and advance protective capital — all exercised subject to the senior loan documents and the recognition agreement.

Recognition Agreement

The senior lender's term sheet expressly contemplates preferred equity from an approved investor and requires a recognition agreement at closing — notice and cure rights, standstill, and permitted transfers of control.
