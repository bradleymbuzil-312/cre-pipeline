Basis

Total cost basis of $18,679,659 ($327,713 per key) against a $12,600,000 loan. The sponsor has funded $2,000,000 of cash equity outside the project basis on top of that.

Amortization

Thirty years, fully amortizing from close with no interest-only period. The balance falls every month at a 10.789% constant while the renovation builds value, so leverage declines from both directions.

Sponsor Support

Completion and cost-overrun guaranty, a full carry guaranty through renovation and ramp, recourse carve-outs and environmental indemnity from the principal.
